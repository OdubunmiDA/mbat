# MBAT limitations

**Numbers referenced here are in `docs/SUMMARY.md` and `data/processed/qa_report.txt`.**

1. **Suppression makes SDUD sums lower bounds.** CMS suppresses NDC-rows with fewer than 11 prescriptions. Over a third of OUD NDC-rows are suppressed, but they are small by construction. The upper-bound columns (`rx_*_upper`) cap how much could be missing, and the share is reported in SUMMARY. Suppression bites hardest in small states and for new products such as extended-release buprenorphine.
2. **SDUD is a reimbursement record, not a patient count.** Prescriptions are claims-level counts. They are not people, days of treatment, or episodes. Prescription length varies by state policy and product, so a state with weekly fills will show more prescriptions than one with 30-day fills for the same patients.
3. **State reporting artifacts are kept as published.** Some state-quarters swing sharply. For example, Oregon 2022Q3 falls to under 1% of its neighbors, and Kentucky jumps more than 100% in 2016Q4 and 2023Q2. These usually reflect managed-care submission timing. They are flagged (`qoq_anomaly_flag`, `mco_reporting_gap`), not corrected, so they reconcile exactly with CMS. Analysts should decide how to handle them.
4. **2026 quarters are preliminary.** The 2026 SDUD file is a first release. National OUD prescriptions in 2026Q1 sit well below 2025Q4 because several large states had incomplete submissions. v1.1 will take the CMS refresh.
5. **Milligrams are computed for transmucosal products only.** Injectable units are reported by CMS in mL (Sublocade 0.5 or 1.5 mL; Brixadi 0.16–0.64 mL), and implant units are kits. Their buprenorphine content is not comparable to daily sublingual doses, so no mg or dose-equivalent is computed.
6. **Pain products are classified by labeled indication.** Butrans, Belbuca and Buprenex (and their generics) are excluded from OUD measures. Off-label OUD use of these products, or pain use of sublingual products, cannot be seen in SDUD.
7. **The two enrollment denominators disagree.** MBES (CMS-64) and the Performance Indicator series measure enrollment differently; PI runs about 9% lower at the median. Neither covers the whole panel (MBES ends 2025Q2; PI starts mid-2017). Rates are provided for each and should not be spliced.
8. **ARCOS ends at 2023Q4 and covers all payers.** DEA's 2024 and 2025 full-year Retail Drug Summary reports do not include buprenorphine (drug code 9064), and DEA gives no reason. An unlisted January–June 2024 report (`report_yr_Jan_Jun2024.pdf`) does include it and is not used in v1.0. ARCOS grams cover every payer and indication, so they are context for Medicaid, not a subset of it. ARCOS values are parsed from PDFs; every row passed a quarters-sum-to-total check, and the author spot-checks pages.
9. **CDC deaths are 12-month-ending and provisional.** Quarterly death counts cannot be recovered exactly from VSRR. Opioid-specific counts are withheld for state-periods with low drug specificity. Recent periods are adjusted by CDC's predicted values, and both reported and predicted values are kept. Deaths are not buprenorphine-specific outcomes.
10. **Policy coverage is partial in v1.0.**
   - Medicaid buprenorphine prior authorization and dose caps are not coded, because no dated 50-state source of Medicaid-specific rules was found. PDAPS explicitly excludes Medicaid-only rules.
   - Expansion dates follow KFF, and Maine and North Carolina differ from CMS notes (see QA §7).
   - Unwinding windows use each state's first termination *reporting* month (the renewal-due month of the first cohort that lost coverage) and the federal 2024-05-31 completion deadline, though some states had extensions. Coverage losses took effect about one month later, so for the 17 states whose start month is the last month of a quarter (and Oregon, three months), `unwinding_in_effect` turns on one quarter before the first person actually lost coverage.
   - Early community-engagement starts are confirmed from state announcements for Nebraska, Montana and Arkansas; Iowa's December 2026 start was still planned when retrieved.
   - The federal community-engagement indicator is coded for ACA expansion states only; demonstration-only states (e.g., Georgia) are not yet coded.
   - Mandatory MOUD benefit exemptions (Hawaii, South Dakota, Wyoming) follow CMS SMD #24-004 and assume the original 2020–2025 period; status after September 2025 is unknown and left blank. This is documented uncertainty: a CMS section 1135 letter (2021-03-19) shows South Dakota submitting a MAT state plan amendment effective 2020-10-01, so its exemption may have been partial (for example, limited to methadone). Analyses using `mandatory_moud_benefit` should test sensitivity to these three states.
11. **Territories are excluded.** Puerto Rico appears in SDUD but is out of scope for v1.0.
12. **Vintage dependence.** CMS re-releases SDUD (2020–2025 were re-released in July 2026). Figures in MBAT match the pinned files in `data/raw/MANIFEST.csv` and may differ from other analyses of other vintages.
